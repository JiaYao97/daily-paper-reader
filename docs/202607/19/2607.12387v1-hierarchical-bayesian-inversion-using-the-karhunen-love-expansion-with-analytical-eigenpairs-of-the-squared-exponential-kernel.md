## 摘要
具有高斯随机场先验的分层贝叶斯反演解决了协方差超参数（如标准差和相关长度）的不确定性问题。当高斯随机场通过Karhunen-Loève（KL）展开表示时，基函数通过协方差核相关的积分特征值问题（IEVP）依赖于这些超参数。因此，每当超参数更新时，必须重复求解IEVP，导致分层推断中显著的计算成本。本文聚焦于平方指数核，并利用高斯加权IEVP的解析解构建KL展开。这种解析KL展开通过消除超参数更新期间IEVP的重复数值求解，提供了比传统KL展开计算效率更高的替代方案。虽然解析KL展开适用于任意域和维度，但它不具备与传统KL展开相同的均方最优性。为解决这一局限性，我们采用基于优化的方法，选择IEVP中高斯权重函数的标准差，以有效减少KL展开的截断误差。一维和二维设置中的数值实验表明，该选择策略为实际应用提供了足够的精度。此外，解析KL展开允许闭式微分，从而通过HMC实现高效的后验采样。所提出的框架应用于稳态达西流模型的贝叶斯反演，其中使用弱信息超先验成功估计了水力传导率场。

## Abstract
Hierarchical Bayesian inversion with Gaussian random field priors addresses uncertainty in covariance hyperparameters, such as the standard deviation and correlation length. When a Gaussian random field is represented by the Karhunen-Loève (KL) expansion, the basis functions depend on these hyperparameters through an integral eigenvalue problem (IEVP) associated with the covariance kernel. Consequently, the IEVP must be solved repeatedly whenever the hyperparameters are updated, leading to significant computational cost in hierarchical inference. In this paper, we focus on the squared exponential kernel and construct the KL expansion using the analytical solution to a Gaussian-weighted IEVP. This analytical KL expansion offers a computationally efficient alternative to the conventional KL expansion by eliminating the repeated numerical solutions of the IEVP during hyperparameter updates. While the analytical KL expansion is applicable to arbitrary domains and dimensions, it does not have the same mean-square optimality as the conventional KL expansion. To address this limitation, we employ an optimization-based approach that selects the standard deviation of the Gaussian weight function in the IEVP to effectively reduce the truncation error of the KL expansion. Numerical experiments in one- and two-dimensional settings show that this selection strategy provides sufficient accuracy for practical applications. Furthermore, the analytical KL expansion admits closed-form differentiation, enabling efficient posterior sampling via HMC. The proposed framework is applied to Bayesian inversion for a steady Darcy flow model, where the hydraulic conductivity field is successfully estimated using weakly informative hyperpriors.
