## 摘要
在PDE约束下，细网格上的系数反演是病态的：稀疏观测对细尺度参数的约束很弱，直接单分辨率优化必须同时恢复所有尺度上的状态场和系数场。这导致收敛速度慢且对初始化敏感；学习的迁移模型需要离线数据，并可能将近似误差引入数值物理中。我们提出了PhyRes-MDNF，一个固定物理的多级离散神经场框架。在每个层级上，单级DNF将反演未知量直接表示为可训练场并优化离散目标。在全空间Darcy实现中，状态场\(U\)及其共享系数场\(K\)在一个固定物理的反演过程中联合优化。在层级之间，一个零初始化的PhyRes-GNN联合执行固定模板延拓和有界残差校正，构建一个输入目标表示，该表示通过固定初始化映射转换为下一个DNF变量。它从观测数据和不变的数值模型中重新拟合，无需离线预训练或细网格真值。因此，粗层级在引入细化自由度之前解析大尺度结构，缩短了细网格优化路径，同时保留了原始离散算子。在相同最终网格更新预算下，多级Darcy实现将系数和状态误差分别降低了约\(85\%\)和\(90\%\)，展示了改进的准确性和最终网格迭代效率。在实测KTC2023 EIT数据上，完整\(W\)流程相比官方线性化CEM重建提高了约\(3.4\%\)的平均Otsu mIoU，相比直接单级DNF提高了\(16.9\%\)。

## Abstract
Coefficient inversion on fine grids under PDE constraints is ill conditioned: sparse observations weakly constrain fine-scale parameters, and direct single-resolution optimization must recover state and coefficient fields across all scales simultaneously. This causes slow, initialization-sensitive convergence; learned transfer models require offline data and can introduce approximation error into the numerical physics. We propose PhyRes-MDNF, a fixed-physics multilevel discrete neural field framework. On each level, a single-level DNF represents the inverse unknowns directly as trainable fields and optimizes the discrete objective. In the full-space Darcy realization, state fields \(U\) and their shared coefficient field \(K\) are optimized jointly in one fixed-physics inverse process. Between levels, one zero-initialized PhyRes-GNN jointly performs fixed-stencil prolongation and bounded residual correction to construct an incoming target representation, which a fixed initialization map converts to the next DNF variables. It is fitted anew from the observations and unchanged numerical model, without offline pretraining or fine-grid truth. Coarse levels therefore resolve large-scale structure before refined degrees of freedom are introduced, shortening the fine-grid optimization path while retaining the original discrete operator. Under the same final-grid update budget, the multilevel Darcy realization reduces coefficient and state errors by approximately \(85\%\) and \(90\%\), respectively, demonstrating improved accuracy and final-grid iteration efficiency. On measured KTC2023 EIT data, the full-\(W\) pipeline improves mean Otsu mIoU by approximately \(3.4\%\) over the official linearized CEM reconstruction and \(16.9\%\) over direct single-level DNF.
