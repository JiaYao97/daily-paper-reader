## 摘要
我们分析了应用于线性耦合抛物型对流-扩散-反应系统的变分物理信息神经网络的训练动力学，当仅有一部分分量经历对流输运时，该系统称为异质的。在神经正切核机制下，时空变分残差上的梯度流简化为一个线性微分系统，其算子是由系统算子的时空符号和矩阵正切核构成的Gram矩阵。主要结果是一个双重筛选定理。在对流占主导的情况下，该矩阵相对于对流分量块的Schur补收敛到一个仅涉及符号的扩散块且无耦合项的表达式，连同正切核的Schur补。由此我们推导出四个推论，即量化筛选耦合能的精确恒等式、由核的典型相关性控制训练速率的退化定律、以Péclet数表示的条件数上界，以及时间频率不参与筛选机制的事实。条件数的增长减慢了共享步长梯度下降，而连续流则没有减速，这使得训练困难归因于优化器而非逼近。我们最终表明，Adam优化器通过其自适应缩放能够缓解这一困难，前提是架构将对流和扩散分量相关的参数分离，这证实了从第二筛选得出的架构性建议。这些预测在二维交换器上以机器精度进行了数值验证，并通过对有限宽度网络的完整训练得到验证。

## Abstract
We analyze the training dynamics of variational physics-informed neural networks applied to linear coupled parabolic convection--diffusion--reaction systems, called heterogeneous when only a subset of the components undergoes convective transport. In the neural tangent kernel regime, the gradient flow on the space-time variational residuals reduces to a linear differential system whose operator is a Gram matrix built from the space-time symbol of the system operator and the matrix tangent kernel. The main result is a double screening theorem. Under dominant convection, the Schur complement of this matrix relative to the block of convective components converges to an expression that involves only the diffusive block of the symbol, with no coupling term, together with the Schur complement of the tangent kernel. From this we derive four consequences, namely an exact identity quantifying the screened coupling energy, a degradation law for the training rate governed by the canonical correlations of the kernel, a bound on the condition number in terms of the Péclet number, and the non-participation of temporal frequencies in the screening mechanism. The growth of the condition number slows down shared-step gradient descent, whereas the continuous flow suffers no slowdown, which makes the training difficulty attributable to the optimizer rather than to the approximation. We finally show that the Adam optimizer, through its adaptive scaling, mitigates this difficulty provided that the architecture separates the parameters associated with the convective and diffusive components, confirming the architectural prescription that follows from the second screening. The predictions are validated numerically down to machine precision, on a two-dimensional exchanger, and by the full training of finite-width networks.
