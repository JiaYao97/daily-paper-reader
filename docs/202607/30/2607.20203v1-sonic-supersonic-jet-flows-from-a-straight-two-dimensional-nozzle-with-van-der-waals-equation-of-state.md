## 摘要
本文关注从二维直喷管射出的声速-超音速射流，该射流由稳态可压缩欧拉系统结合范德瓦尔斯状态方程描述。在喷管出口处规定流动状态，而周围介质假设为真空或压力较低的静态大气。当流动在喷管出口达到声速状态时，控制方程变为退化双曲型，由此产生的射流问题可表述为退化自由边界问题。喷管出口端点附近的奇异行为进一步增加了分析的复杂性。通过采用特征分解和适当的先验估计，我们建立了扩展至真空的局部Lipschitz连续声速-超音速射流的全局存在性。此外，在存在压力低于喷管出口压力的静态大气时，我们证明了喷管出口附近声速-超音速射流的局部存在性。

## Abstract
This article concerns sonic-supersonic jet flows issuing from a two-dimensional straight nozzle described by the steady compressible Euler system under the van der Waals equation of state. The flow state is prescribed at the nozzle exit, while the surrounding medium is assumed to be either a vacuum or a static atmosphere with lower pressure. When the flow reaches the sonic state at the nozzle exit, the governing equations become degenerate hyperbolic and the resulting jet flow problem can be formulated as a degenerate free boundary problem. The analysis is further complicated by singular behavior near the endpoints of the nozzle exit. By employing characteristic decompositions and suitable a priori estimates, we establish the global existence of locally Lipschitz continuous sonic-supersonic jet flows expanding into a vacuum. Moreover, in the presence of a static atmosphere with pressure lower than that at the nozzle exit, we prove the local existence of sonic-supersonic jet flows near the nozzle exit.
