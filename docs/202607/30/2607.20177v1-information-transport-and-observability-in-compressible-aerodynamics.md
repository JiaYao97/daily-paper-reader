## 摘要
压力测量提供了可压缩空气动力学流动的稀疏但直接的观测，然而关于隐藏的空气动力学参数的信息如何通过流动传输并编码在这些观测中仍然鲜为人知。本文利用可微激波捕捉浸入边界求解器研究可压缩空气动力学中的信息传输与可观测性。通过在整个非定常流解中传播梯度，引入了一种基于自动微分的可观测性度量，以量化稀疏压力测量对未知空气动力学参数的灵敏度，并识别用于逆学习的信息传感位置。结果表明，空气动力学信息在流场中非均匀传输，产生局部高可观测性区域。逆学习实验进一步表明，可观测性与可学习性是相关但不同的概念：尽管高可观测性探针通常促进准确的参数恢复，但最高可观测性探针并非始终是参数推断最有效的。此外，流动状态和翼型几何形状均显著影响可观测性的分布和逆学习的收敛行为。这些发现建立了一个定量框架，用于理解空气动力学信息如何在稀疏测量中编码，并展示了自动微分在可观测性分析、信息传感器选择和空气动力学逆分析中的潜力。

## Abstract
Pressure measurements provide sparse but direct observations of compressible aerodynamic flows, yet how information about hidden aerodynamic parameters is transported through the flow and encoded in these observations remains poorly understood. Here, we investigate information transport and observability in compressible aerodynamics using a differentiable shock-capturing immersed-boundary solver. By propagating gradients through the full unsteady flow solution, an automatic-differentiation-based observability metric is introduced to quantify the sensitivity of sparse pressure measurements to unknown aerodynamic parameters and identify informative sensing locations for inverse learning. The results reveal that aerodynamic information is transported non-uniformly through the flow field, producing localized regions of high observability. Inverse-learning experiments further demonstrate that observability and learnability are related but distinct concepts: although highly observable probes generally facilitate accurate parameter recovery, the highest-observability probe is not consistently the most effective for parameter inference. Furthermore, both the flow regime and the airfoil geometry substantially influence the distribution of observability and the convergence behavior of inverse learning. These findings establish a quantitative framework for understanding how aerodynamic information is encoded in sparse measurements and demonstrate the potential of automatic differentiation for observability analysis, informative sensor selection, and aerodynamic inverse analysis.
